◐ malem a travel companion that understands you

Travel that fits you, not the algorithm's idea of you.

Tell malem what you actually care about — pace, budget, food, faith, family, accessibility, and what to avoid. Then pick a vibe and get an itinerary that respects all of it. Every preference is a control you set, not an assumption we make.

1. Personal profile

Vibes, budget, pace, dietary, accessibility, religious & cultural, modesty, medical, family, and places to avoid — all editable.

2. Vibe itineraries

Live Like a Local, Iconic First Visit, Hidden Gems, Halal Food & Culture, and more. Pick one, blend two, or slide the vibe.

3. Packing planner

Forecast, activities, laundry, luggage, medication, faith attire, adapters — split into pack, buy, rent, and do-not-pack.

4. Discover Now

Three hours, light rain at six, twelve minutes from a covered market — here are three plans.

5. Local discovery

Small businesses, neighborhood favorites, seasonal events. "Hidden gem" judged by locals, not just popularity.

6. What to Expect

Etiquette, tipping, prayer facilities, transit norms, common scams. Dated, sourced, and labeled by confidence.

7. Group & journal

Collaborators vote on vibes; each traveler gets their own packing list. Publish what you actually did.
